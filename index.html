01 — About Us

Three souls.
One broken sound.

Born in a cramped garage with a borrowed amp and a guitar missing its high E, Broken Strings turned a mistake into a manifesto.

Since 2016 we've blended raw rock, blues grit and cinematic synths into songs that hit hard and stay long. Three albums, 400+ shows and countless snapped strings later — we're still chasing the noise that made us start.
